# Lite3 handstand-walk: high-frequency front-foot contact — diagnosis & fixes

Source: multi-agent diagnosis workflow `wf_efca09c1` (5 lenses → consolidate → adversarial verify → synthesize), 2026-06-13.

## Diagnosis

The high-frequency front-paw buzz + small swing is an over-determined LEARNED local optimum, not a mechanical limit (geometry, joint range, 30 Nm actuators, and 50 Hz rate are all confirmed adequate — H11). The dominant, confirmed root cause is an observability gap: the ONLY rhythm-shaping reward in the V15 lineage, handstand_feet_clearance (sinusoidal, cycle_time=1.5 s), keys on an internal wall-clock phase `(episode_length_buf*step_dt)%cycle_time/cycle_time` (rewards.py:1266) that the actor CANNOT see — the actor obs is just 6 phase-free terms x10 frames (env_cfgs.py:61-68), and 10 frames = 0.2 s << the 1.5 s cycle, so phase is unreconstructable. The IsaacGym source this was ported from fed sin/cos phase into the actor obs and phase-matched its contact reward; the mjlab port kept the clocked reward but dropped the clock. A policy blind to phase can only maximize the phase-AVERAGED expectation, which collapses to "keep both front feet near the ground and twitch" — exactly the observed shuffle. This is compounded by phase-free contact rewards that any frequency satisfies, an air-time term that is a net-negative barrier, and amplitude-suppressing energy/accel penalties. The energy finetune did nothing because it adds one damping cost on a converged shuffle basin (resume from model_14999) and was ported from a pure-posture, zero-velocity task where energy aligns with stillness — an invalid transfer to a velocity-commanded walk.

**Primary root cause:** H1 — the gait-phase clock is invisible to the actor. handstand_feet_clearance (the V15 rhythm driver, weight 1.5, cycle_time 1.5 s) grades the policy against an internal episode-time phase, but RLDEPLOY_ACTOR_TERM_ORDER (env_cfgs.py:61-68) contains no sin/cos clock term, even though an identical-formula `gait_clock` obs fn already exists (observations.py:9-17) and is wired into go2_trot. With no observable phase, the deterministic policy cannot time which front foot to lift when, so it converges to a phase-averaged micro-shuffle. This is the structural blocker that every prior amplitude/penalty experiment (V3/V5/V8/V10/V12/V14) failed to escape because none of them restored the missing clock.

## Recommended actions (prioritized)

### [1] Add the existing gait_clock sin/cos to BOTH actor and critic obs of the Stride-V15 task, with cycle_time matched to feet_clearance, and RETRAIN FROM SCRATCH (do not resume model_14999).

- **Rationale:** PRIMARY confirmed root cause (H1, H11). handstand_feet_clearance keys on phase=(episode_length_buf*step_dt)%cycle_time/cycle_time (rewards.py:1266) which is identical to gait_clock's formula (observations.py:14) — so feeding gait_clock(cycle_time=1.5) closes the loop: the reward now grades against a phase the actor can read, converting the phase-averaged shuffle into a deterministic alternation at 1/1.5 Hz. The IsaacGym source that produced clean stepping did exactly this (sin/cos in obs + phase-keyed reward). go2_trot already demonstrates the wiring pattern.
- **Concrete changes:** In go2_mjlab/config/lite3_handstand_rldeploy/env_cfgs.py: (a) append "gait_clock" to RLDEPLOY_ACTOR_TERM_ORDER (env_cfgs.py:61-68) and add it to the terms dict in _rldeploy_actor_terms() (env_cfgs.py:95-120) as ObservationTermCfg(func=go2_mdp.gait_clock, params={"cycle_time": 1.5}) — MUST be 1.5 to match feet_clearance cycle_time set at env_cfgs.py:723, NOT the 0.5 default in observations.py:11; (b) since critic_terms = {**actor_terms, ...} (env_cfgs.py:130-140) the clock is inherited by the critic automatically; (c) bump RLDEPLOY_SINGLE_OBS_DIM 45->47 (env_cfgs.py:70) so RLDEPLOY_ACTOR_OBS_DIM becomes 47*10=470. Train fresh (rl_cfg.py: do NOT set resume/load_checkpoint to model_14999) — the input contract changed and the shuffle basin must not be inherited. NOTE: this breaks the 45-dim rl_deploy_handstand C++ input contract; the deployed runner must be extended to compute and feed the same sin/cos(2*pi*t/1.5) clock, or keep a separate non-deploy variant.
- **Expected effect:** Policy can deterministically schedule FL-up/FR-down vs FR-up/FL-down, converting the high-frequency micro-shuffle into one clean alternation per 1.5 s cycle (lower frequency, larger amplitude). This is the change every prior version was missing.
- **Risk:** Input-size change breaks the deploy contract (must extend C++ runner or use a non-deploy variant). Requires full retrain (~15k iters), not a cheap resume. cycle_time mismatch (leaving 0.5) would feed a clock inconsistent with the reward and be worse than nothing — must set 1.5.

### [2] Make the contact/stance rewards phase-consistent and de-farmable: adopt the already-existing moving-gated alternation terms instead of the phase-free single_stance/stance_air stack.

- **Rationale:** H4/H5 confirmed-partial: the V15 contact terms (single_stance_contact +0.1 = handstand_contact n_contact==1, rewards.py:1113-1133; stance_air_penalty -5.0) are phase-free instantaneous-state rewards that ANY frequency satisfies, so even with the clock the policy can farm single_stance with a 1-2 step flick. A moving-gated alternation set ALREADY EXISTS in the quiet_step variant (env_cfgs.py:1067-1084): moving_single_stance_contact +2.5, moving_both_front_feet_air -2.0, moving_double_front_contact -0.5 — but it is NOT in the V15 lineage. Pairing these (or a phase-matched contact reward) with the observable clock from rank 1 closes the reward loop the way IsaacGym's _reward_contact did.
- **Concrete changes:** In the V15 (or a new V16) cfg in env_cfgs.py: raise single_stance_contact weight from 0.1 (set at env_cfgs.py:675) toward ~1.5, OR import the quiet_step block (env_cfgs.py:1067-1084) adding moving_single_stance_contact (handstand_contact, weight ~2.0, foot_indices (0,1), command_name twist) and moving_double_front_contact (handstand_moving_double_stance_contact, weight ~-0.5) so continuous double-support is penalized only while moving. Reduce stance_air_penalty magnitude from -5.0 (env_cfgs.py:623) to ~-1.5 so a brief both-airborne frame during a committed stride is not 50x the single-stance bonus. Keep all of these gated by _handstand_moving_command_mask so zero-command stability is preserved.
- **Expected effect:** Rewards a sustained single-support half-cycle and penalizes continuous double-support while moving, so the optimum becomes plant-one / swing-other for the full half-cycle rather than a buzz that keeps exactly-one-down on average.
- **Risk:** Lowering stance_air_penalty too far can re-open the both-front-airborne hop the NoHop terms fought; keep handstand_feet_on_air (+1.0) and lin_vel_z penalties as anchors. moving_double_stance_contact was flagged 'toxic' in earlier docstrings if applied without the clock — only add it together with rank 1.

### [3] Fix the air-time term so it is a positive ramp from an ACHIEVABLE swing duration instead of a net-negative barrier at 0.4 s, and lower its weight.

- **Rationale:** H2 confirmed: rewards.py:1222 rew=sum((air_time-0.4)*first_contact) is UNCLAMPED, so every sub-0.4 s touchdown pays a penalty; telemetry shows it net-negative the entire 15k-iter run (-0.047, max -0.0006, 0/15000 iters positive) at weight 3.0 (env_cfgs.py:744). It actively punishes the short lifts the policy makes, pinning the gait to ground-skimming, with NO positive pull until 0.4 s (which the policy never reaches).
- **Concrete changes:** In go2_mjlab/mdp/rewards.py:1222 replace `rew = torch.sum((self.air_time - 0.4) * first_contact, dim=1)` with a capped positive ramp from an achievable threshold, e.g. `rew = torch.sum(torch.clamp(torch.minimum(self.air_time, torch.tensor(0.6)) - 0.15, min=0.0) * first_contact, dim=1)` (pays from 0.15 s = ~8 steps, caps at 0.6 s to prevent a one-leg hop). In env_cfgs.py:744 DROP feet_air_time weight from 3.0 to ~0.5-1.0 when flipping it positive, so it does not re-create the V2/V4 low-threshold micro-shuffle farm.
- **Expected effect:** Lifting a front foot becomes mildly rewarded from a reachable duration rather than punished, giving a positive gradient toward longer swings; the cap prevents degenerating into a single perpetual hop.
- **Risk:** A low threshold at high weight re-creates the V2/V4 flutter-farm — that is why weight must drop to ~0.5-1.0 and the cap must stay. Only effective combined with rank 1 (the long swing must be schedulable).

### [4] Remove or standing-command-gate the energy penalty and relax the amplitude-suppressing accel penalties in the moving regime.

- **Rationale:** H7/H8 confirmed: energy (-0.003, env_cfgs.py:1127-1130) and the inherited dof_acc=-1.0e-3 / action_rate_l2=-0.10 (env_cfgs.py:809-810, 4x/2x the base) are all monotone in joint-velocity/power and CANNOT distinguish good large-slow swing from bad fast buzz — |qvel|*|tau| peaks exactly at the slow stride reversals (PD Kp=40, scale 0.25). The energy finetune was ported from pure-posture Go1Handstand where energy aligns with stillness; on a velocity-commanded walk it just damps amplitude and, added on a converged shuffle via resume, gave PPO no reason to leave the basin (explains '表现还是一样的').
- **Concrete changes:** Do NOT carry energy=-0.003 through stride-learning: remove env_cfgs.py:1127-1130 from the finetune (or command-gate it by multiplying by the standing mask, mirroring zero_joint_vel/handstand_joint_vel_zero at rewards.py:1339-1351). Relax dof_acc from -1.0e-3 toward -5.0e-4 and action_rate_l2 from -0.10 toward -0.07 (one step, not all the way to base -2.5e-4/-0.05) — prefer command-gating to standing envs so the standing regime keeps full sim2real smoothing. Re-add a small energy term ONLY as a stage-2 polish AFTER a clean alternation exists.
- **Expected effect:** Frees the high-power takeoff/landing a deliberate larger stride needs while keeping idle jitter priced at zero command. Removes the counterproductive damping that the finetune added.
- **Risk:** Cutting accel penalties too hard hurts sim2real smoothness and can re-open the hop; relax in one step and prefer command-gating. Energy removal alone does NOT create stride — it only stops suppressing it; must accompany ranks 1-3.

### [5] Decouple the dense tracking reward from front-stance contact by removing stance_foot_indices from the tracking soft-gate (or raising support_floor).

- **Rationale:** H6 confirmed-present: the four tracking terms multiply raw reward by _handstand_soft_quality whose support factor folds in front-feet contact (rewards.py:528-536, stance_foot_indices=(0,1) wired at env_cfgs.py:201/soft_quality_params). With support_floor=0.05, lifting one front foot scales tracking reward to 0.05+0.95*0.5=0.525 — nearly halving the primary locomotion reward during every swing, a gradient toward minimal front-foot lift. The harsher sibling (contact mean) was already removed in V6, so this is the residual of the same effect.
- **Concrete changes:** In go2_mjlab/config/lite3_handstand_rldeploy/env_cfgs.py: either remove stance_foot_indices from the support_params dict used by the tracking soft-gates (keep orientation+base_height+rear_air+body_clearance), OR raise support_floor from 0.05 toward ~0.6 in the soft_quality_params so a single-foot swing only modestly reduces tracking reward. The front-contact requirement is then expressed only via the dedicated single_stance/alternation terms from rank 2. stance_air_penalty (command-independent) still guards against hops, so this does not re-open the hop exploit.
- **Expected effect:** A front-foot swing no longer halves the velocity-tracking reward, removing the gradient that rewards keeping both front feet planted.
- **Risk:** Secondary/contributing, not the primary blocker; effect is bounded and should be paired with ranks 1-3, not applied alone. Removing the support factor slightly weakens the gate's anti-prone-farming guard, but stance_air_penalty covers hops.

## What to avoid

DO NOT expect any energy-only finetune to fix this — it was REFUTED in practice ('表现还是一样的'): energy was ported from mujoco_playground Go1Handstand, a PURE-POSTURE zero-velocity task where energy aligns with stillness; on a velocity-commanded walk it only damps amplitude and, resumed on a converged shuffle (model_14999), cannot leave the basin (H7/H8). DO NOT just swap the sinusoidal feet_clearance for handstand_swing_foot_height_linear as the primary amplitude reward — that EXACT recipe was already run as Stride-V10 and measured WORSE (swing height ~2.2% of ideal vs V15's ~9%, still net-negative air-time); at high weight it also admits a zero-frequency one-leg-up-forever exploit. DO NOT add a phase-matched contact reward (H4) WITHOUT first making phase observable (rank 1) — on an unobservable clock it becomes a randomly-toggling target the policy maximizes in expectation (~0.5 regardless of frequency), reintroducing the exact failure. DO NOT widen joint ranges, raise base height 'for clearance', or change decimation/timestep — geometry, 30 Nm actuators, and 50 Hz are all confirmed adequate (H11), and changing 50 Hz breaks the rl_deploy C++ contract. DO NOT re-introduce a front-HipY default-pose pull (H10 refuted) — the soft limit never gates the action (the actuator is a MuJoCo position actuator clamped only to the hard range), V14+ deliberately REMOVED the front legs from default_pos to unblock swing, and front HipY has zero static pull in the running V15/Finetune checkpoint. DO NOT rely on the handstand quality gate for anything — _handstand_quality is hardwired to return 2.0 (rewards.py:813-830), so every 'quality>0.70' gate is a no-op. DO NOT reduce zero_stance_contact's reach into the moving phase (H5 fix #3) — it is already standing-command-gated (rewards.py:1321-1336) and never fires while moving, so that edit is a no-op.

## Verified hypotheses (evidence)

### H1-no-phase-clock-in-actor-obs
- **Root cause:** The actor observation contains NO gait-phase / clock signal, yet the entire stride-shaping reward stack is phase-locked to wall-clock time the actor cannot see. Actor obs = the 6 terms in RLDEPLOY_ACTOR_TERM_ORDER (env_cfgs.py:31-38: base_ang_vel, projected_gravity, commands, joint_pos, joint_vel, last_action) = 45 dims x 10-frame history (env_cfgs.py:40-42); none is a phase/clock. The IsaacGym reference this was ported from puts sin_phase/cos_phase at the FRONT of both actor and critic obs (Lite3_handstand.py:197-221, num_observations=48 = 'sin cos command + 45', Config:7). A gait_clock obs fn already EXISTS but is wired into neither group (observations.py:9-17); it IS used by go2_trot/go2_stairs/go2_jump (go2_trot/env_cfgs.py:145-150).
- **Evidence:** VERIFIED: env_cfgs.py:31-38 RLDEPLOY_ACTOR_TERM_ORDER (6 terms, no phase); :40 RLDEPLOY_SINGLE_OBS_DIM=45; _rldeploy_actor_terms() :65-90; observations.py:9-17 gait_clock defined; :11 phase=(episode_length_buf*step_dt)%cycle_time/cycle_time but never registered. feet_clearance keys on the same hidden phase (rewards.py:1266). 10-frame history = 0.2 s << 1.5 s cycle, so the actor cannot even reconstruct phase from history. IsaacGym Lite3_handstand.py:96-99 period=1.2 offset=0.5; :197-221 sin/cos prepended to actor+critic.
- **Fix:** Add the existing observations.gait_clock (sin/cos) to BOTH actor and critic obs of the RLDeploy task, exactly as go2_trot does and as the IsaacGym reference does, with cycle_time matching feet_clearance (1.5 s in V15; 1.2 s matches the reference). Append to RLDEPLOY_ACTOR_TERM_ORDER, bump RLDEPLOY_SINGLE_OBS_DIM 45->47 and ACTOR_OBS_DIM. With phase observable, the policy can deterministically time FL-up/FR-down vs FR-up/FL-down, converting the phase-averaged shuffle into a clean alternating stride at 1/cycle_time. NOTE: this is a policy-contract change (input size grows), so it requires retrain from scratch (or warm-start with re-expanded obs), and the deployed C++ rl_deploy_handstand input vector must be extended to feed the same clock at runtime.

### H2-feet-air-time-thresholded-net-negative
- **Root cause:** feet_air_time (front feet, V15 weight 3.0 — the single largest swing-encouraging term) scores sum((air_time - 0.4) * first_contact) UN-CLAMPED, so any touchdown after a swing shorter than 0.4 s pays a PENALTY. The measured episode contribution is NET NEGATIVE (-0.0469 final, max only -0.0006) over 15000 iters: the policy's front-foot swings are almost always <0.4 s, so this large-weight term actively punishes the micro-shuffle it produces — but the punishment is small enough that the policy keeps the shuffle and just minimizes air time, pinning the gait to ground-skimming.
- **Evidence:** VERIFIED: rewards.py:1222 rew = torch.sum((self.air_time - 0.4) * first_contact, dim=1) — no clamp; weight 3.0 (env_cfgs.py:714); step_dt=0.02 s (timestep 0.001 * decimation 20, env_cfgs.py:193-194) so 0.4 s = 20 control steps. Measured: lionrock run 2026-05-20_15-15-26 Episode_Reward/feet_air_time -0.0011 -> -0.0469 (max -0.0006). Stride-V2/V4 docstring (env_cfgs.py:598-613) records the policy 'farmed single_stance_contact by lifting front feet for only a couple of physics steps (~0.04 s), yielding a rapid micro-shuffle gait'.
- **Fix:** Replace the thresholded form with a monotone-positive swing-duration reward that pays from t=0 and is never negative: rew = sum(clamp(air_time - 0.4, min=0) * first_contact) at rewards.py:1222, OR reward min(air_time, T_target) on first contact. Lower the implicit threshold toward the achievable swing (~0.15-0.2 s = 8-10 steps, or cycle_time/2 - margin) so it pays before a full 0.4 s. Best combined with H1 so the long swing can actually be scheduled.

### H3-clearance-sinusoid-fights-and-pays-for-no-lift
- **Root cause:** The only phase-aware shaping term, handstand_feet_clearance (V15 weight 1.5, cycle_time 1.5 s, target 0.25, sharpness 50), is doubly broken: (a) its exp(-sharpness*|z-target|) profile and the |sin| target that vanishes at the cycle midpoints both pay a large fraction of reward at z=0, so 'barely lifting' farms most of the reward without amplitude; and (b) its internal phase is invisible to the actor (see H1) AND incompatible with the unclocked contact terms, so it acts as near-uniform noise the blind policy averages over. Measured contribution is tiny (0.083 of a 1.5-weight term) and the docstrings record a persistent plateau across versions.
- **Evidence:** VERIFIED: rewards.py:1266-1272 phase internal, target=|sin(2pi*phase)|*target_foot_height, swing_mask phase>=0.5 / <0.5, reward exp(-|z-target|*sharpness). V15 target_foot_height=0.25 sharpness=50 (env_cfgs.py:868-869), cycle_time 1.5 (env_cfgs.py:693), weight 1.5 (env_cfgs.py:715). Docstrings: V8 'stalled at ~0.36/1.0' (:703-709), V12 'pays ~64% reward at z=0' (:987-991), V14 'sharpness 20 target 0.15 still gives ~64% at z=0' (:852-863). Measured: lionrock run 2026-05-20_15-15-26 Episode_Reward/feet_clearance +0.0008 -> +0.0829 (max +0.0928). The non-farmable linear alternative ALREADY EXISTS: handstand_swing_foot_height_linear (rewards.py:1279-1318) clamp(z/cap) only while airborne, 0 at z=0; used in V10.
- **Fix:** Drop the sinusoidal feet_clearance and make handstand_swing_foot_height_linear (rewards.py:1279-1318, cap_height 0.25) the PRIMARY amplitude reward, raising its weight above the residual cost sum — it is 0 at z=0 and cannot be farmed without lifting. If a clock is added (H1), the swing window can be gated by the OBSERVABLE clock instead of an internal one. Pair with the corrected air-time term (H2) so amplitude and frequency are shaped by complementary, non-farmable signals.

### H4-contact-rewards-unclocked-not-phase-matched
- **Root cause:** The mjlab port DROPPED the reference's phase-keyed per-leg contact reward and replaced it with contact terms that score only RAW INSTANTANEOUS contact state with NO time/phase reference: handstand_contact rewards (n_contact==1), stance_contact_mean rewards mean contact, stance_air_penalty penalizes both-airborne. None reads a clock. So nothing rewards a SPECIFIC alternation rhythm — the policy is free to pick ANY frequency that keeps 'exactly one foot down' true on average, and a 20 Hz flicker scores similarly to a 0.8 Hz stride. The reference (IsaacGym _reward_contact) instead rewards each foot for contact==schedule using leg_phase with a 0.5 offset, which is what made it step cleanly.
- **Evidence:** VERIFIED: handstand_contact (rewards.py:1113-1133) returns (n_contact==1) gated only by quality+moving_mask, no phase; stance_contact_mean (rewards.py:1101-1110) mean contact; stance_air_penalty (rewards.py:1154-1172) (n_contact==0), no command/clock gate. IsaacGym _reward_contact (Lite3_handstand.py:954-960): is_stance = leg_phase[:,i] < 0.51; contact = forces>1; res += ~(contact ^ is_stance) — per-foot contact-vs-schedule with leg_phase offset 0.5 making FL/FR alternate.
- **Fix:** Add a phase-keyed contact reward for the FRONT pair (0,1) porting IsaacGym _reward_contact: phase=(episode_length_buf*step_dt)%cycle/cycle; is_stance_0 = phase<0.5; is_stance_1 = ((phase+0.5)%1)<0.5; reward += ~(contact_i ^ is_stance_i) for FL,FR; gated by the moving command; SAME cycle_time as the new obs clock. Weight ~0.3 (reference value). This is binary and far easier to optimize than a height profile. It REQUIRES H1 (the clock must be observable) to be effective.

### H5-anti-plant-imbalance-single-stance-too-weak-farmable
- **Root cause:** The reward budget overwhelmingly favors 'both front feet planted'. The only term positively rewarding a stepping (single-support) state, single_stance_contact (handstand_contact), is weight 0.1-0.3 and is an instantaneous per-frame STATE reward with no minimum duration, so a 1-2 step flick farms it; meanwhile zero_stance_contact +3.0, tracking_lin_vel (~1.34 measured), and the anti-hop stance_air_penalty -5.0 dominate. A real big swing risks a both-airborne frame costing 50x the single-stance bonus, so the risk-adjusted optimum is to keep both feet grounded and flick one foot a few mm for a few ms.
- **Evidence:** VERIFIED weights: single_stance_contact 0.3 (env_cfgs.py:617) then 0.1 (env_cfgs.py:645), inherited; stance_air_penalty -5.0 (env_cfgs.py:593); zero_stance_contact +3.0 (env_cfgs.py:498). handstand_contact fires per-frame for (n_contact==1) with no duration (rewards.py:1113-1133). Measured (lionrock 2026-05-20_15-15-26): tracking_lin_vel +1.3398 (max +2.265), tracking_ang_vel +0.8518, zero_stance_contact +0.7866, default_pos_reward +0.2869 vs single_stance_contact +0.0210, feet_clearance +0.0829, feet_air_time -0.0469. Stride-V4 docstring (env_cfgs.py:598-613): policy 'farmed single_stance_contact by lifting front feet for only ~0.04 s'.
- **Fix:** Rebalance and de-farm: (1) gate single_stance_contact on a minimum continuous single-stance duration (>=~6 steps) OR replace with the phase-matched contact reward of H4 so it cannot be farmed by flutter; raise its weight toward 1.5. (2) Reduce stance_air_penalty magnitude (-5.0 -> ~-1.5) so a brief both-airborne frame during a committed stride is not catastrophic. (3) Reduce zero_stance_contact's reach into the moving phase so planting is not rewarded while commanded to move.

### H6-soft-gate-couples-tracking-to-front-stance-contact
- **Root cause:** The dense locomotion reward (the four tracking terms) is multiplied by _handstand_soft_quality, whose support factor folds in the FRONT (stance) feet being in contact and whose rear-air factor down-weights frames where rear feet drop. With support_floor=0.05, lifting one front foot for a stride drops support_quality from 1.0 to 0.5 and scales tracking reward by (0.05 + 0.95*0.5) = 0.525 — nearly HALVING the primary locomotion reward during every swing. This couples 'walk faster' to 'keep stance feet down', directly opposing the foot-lift a stride needs.
- **Evidence:** VERIFIED: _handstand_soft_quality (rewards.py:483-537): pose_quality *= (0.20 + 0.80*rear_air_quality) at :527; then *= (support_floor + (1-floor)*support_quality) at :534-536; support_quality folds in _handstand_stance_contact_quality when stance_foot_indices given (:472-477, via _handstand_support_quality :528-532). stance_foot_indices=(0,1) wired into the tracking soft-gates (env_cfgs.py:159-166,206-210 support_params). support_floor=0.05 (env_cfgs.py:325).
- **Fix:** Decouple tracking quality from front-stance contact: remove stance_foot_indices from the tracking soft-gate support_params (keep only orientation + base_height + rear_air + body_clearance in the gate), OR raise support_floor toward ~0.6 so a single-foot swing only modestly reduces tracking reward. Express the front-contact requirement instead via the dedicated single_stance/phase-matched terms (H4/H5) where it can reward alternation rather than continuous double support.

### H7-energy-and-velderiv-penalties-suppress-the-swing
- **Root cause:** Three co-linear penalties all monotone-increasing in joint-velocity magnitude/derivative jointly price out the high-power motion a deliberate stride requires, while being UNABLE to distinguish good high-amplitude/low-frequency motion from the bad high-frequency buzz (they penalize TOTAL motion). The pre-existing dof_acc -1.0e-3 and action_rate_l2 -0.10 (both inherited from Stride-V10 through V15) already cap takeoff/landing accelerations; the V15-Finetune adds energy -0.003 (sum|qvel|*|tau| over all 12 joints) co-linear on top. Because energy is monotone in power, the lowest-energy nearby behavior to a small shuffle is an EVEN SMALLER shuffle, so the finetune mildly REINFORCES the micro-shuffle rather than escaping it.
- **Evidence:** VERIFIED: energy fn rewards.py:1387-1402 sum(|qvel|*|qfrc_actuator|); finetune adds ONLY energy=-0.003 on joint_names='.*' (env_cfgs.py:1097-1100), structure otherwise identical to V15. dof_acc=-1.0e-3 + action_rate_l2=-0.10 set in Stride-V10 (env_cfgs.py:779-780), inherited V11->V15; finetune docstring (:1088-1090) confirms dof_acc 'NOT bumped ... Stride-V15 already inherits -1.0e-3 (stronger)'. Stride-V8/V10 docstrings (env_cfgs.py:763-767) blame these: 'dof_acc and action_rate_l2 make rapid joint motion expensive — capping the speed of takeoff/landing'. PD authority Kp=40, action_scale HipY/Knee=0.25 (lite3_constants.py:110-126,318-322) so swing torque is substantial and energy's product peaks during the swing.
- **Fix:** Energy is the wrong knob for the WALKING regime — remove it (delete env_cfgs.py:1097-1100) OR command-gate it to standing envs only (multiply by the standing-command mask, mirroring handstand_joint_vel_zero rewards.py:1339-1351) so it kills idle jitter at zero command but leaves the commanded stride un-penalized. Also relax the velocity-derivative penalties in the moving regime: cut dof_acc toward the base -2.5e-4 (env_cfgs.py:288-289) and action_rate_l2 toward -0.05, or command-gate them to standing envs. For idle quiet use the already-present command-gated handstand_joint_vel_zero + zero_stance_contact. ### H8-playground-recipe-mismatch-pure-posture
- **Root cause:** The energy=-0.003 finetune was ported from mujoco_playground Go1Handstand, which is a PURE-POSTURE task with NO velocity command and NO stepping objective — it even adds _cost_stay_still penalizing base velocity and a _cost_contact that PENALIZES the lifted feet touching (it WANTS feet glued in a static pose). Transferring it to a velocity-commanded walk (UniformVelocityCommand twist, ~60-75% moving envs) is an invalid transfer: what is free in posture is destructive in a walk, because here the task reward is maximized by periodic motion so the energy gradient points OPPOSITE the stepping gradient.
- **Evidence:** Go1Handstand handstand.py: default_config has no command field; comment line 64 'For finetuning, use energy=-0.003 and dof_acc=-2.5e-7'; _cost_stay_still :352-353 penalizes qvel; _cost_contact :367-373 returns any(feet_contact) over LIFTED feet, scale -0.1 (penalty for swing-foot touch). ### H9-single-support-balance-is-the-stable-trap
- **Root cause:** Front-paw handstand stance is a near-line support TRANSVERSE to the walking direction with the CoM ~0.39 m above it on spherical (point-contact, no ankle torque) feet. Lifting one front foot collapses support to a single point and forces the lone foot to balance the whole inverted body in both roll and pitch with no ankle authority while the swing leg's momentum perturbs it. Keeping both feet planted/near-ground (micro-shuffle) is the stable local optimum that never enters this single-support balance crisis — so the high contact frequency is the CONSEQUENCE of never committing to the unstable single-support phase, and the anti-hop/keep-planted rewards reinforce that both-down basin.
- **Evidence:** FK of the two front feet at the open desire pose: FL=(-0.093,+0.159,0.014), FR=(-0.093,-0.159,0.014) — lateral y separation 0.318 m but fore-aft x separation 0.000 m (a line transverse to walking). Torso mass 5.6 kg at base 0.39 (lite3.xml:45, env_cfgs.py:70 LITE3_BASE_HEIGHT_TARGET). Spherical FOOT bodies (lite3.xml:74), no ankle pitch torque. The actuator is NOT the limiter: action_scale 0.25 demands ~10 Nm of the ~30 Nm limit (Kp=40, lite3_constants.py:110-126,318-322); a free swing leg accelerates >1000 rad/s^2. anti-hop terms stance_air_penalty/zero_stance/single_stance reinforce both-down (env_cfgs.py:462,498,583).
- **Fix:** Lower the cost/risk of single support: (1) give the FOOT collision a small capsule/box footprint (currently a sphere, lite3.xml:74) or raise foot friction so the planted foot has a little fore-aft/roll authority during single support; (2) make 'both feet down' actually LOSE reward during the swing window via the phase-gated contact reward (H4); (3) curriculum — keep commands starting at 0 (already does) but ramp a MINIMUM step amplitude so the policy is forced through single support early when episodes are short and cheap to fail. Secondary: a modest base-height raise (0.39 -> ~0.45-0.47, toward the IsaacGym base_height_target=0.52, Config:156) lengthens the stance leg so a swing is less of a relative collapse.

### H11-geometry-and-control-rate-are-NOT-the-limiter
